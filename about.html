What we grow & sell

Naturally grown agricultural produce, cold-pressed oils, Kerala spices, Kashmiri dry fruits, and traditional sweets & snacks.

Signature staples include Kala Namak Rice, Yellow Jowar, Salem Turmeric Powder, Paigambari Wheat, and Khapli Wheat.

How we prepare

Oils are cold-pressed with care. Sweets and snacks are made the honest way — without artificial colours, essence, preservatives, or silver leaf.

From soil to shelf, the aim is simple: food that is sourced responsibly and prepared honestly.
