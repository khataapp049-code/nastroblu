Our Story

From conscious choices at home to our first store.

The idea of Nastro Blu came in 2021–22 when we started making conscious choices about the food we ate and the way we lived — exploring traditional practices, naturally grown produce, indigenous grains, and better-quality ingredients.

We made our own desi cow ghee using the traditional Bilona method and went beyond labels — visiting farms and understanding where our food truly came from. What began as a small journey from home grew into a larger purpose: better ingredients, traditional wisdom, and honest preparation for everyday food.

On 5th September 2025, that vision took its next step with the opening of our first Nastro Blu store. Nastro Blu means “Blue Ribbon” — excellence, quality and distinction.
