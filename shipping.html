Policies

Shipping & Delivery Policy

At Nastro Blu, we take great care to ensure that your products are packed and delivered in the best possible condition.

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days, subject to product availability.

Delivery timelines (quick guide)

  • Sweets & snacks — Hyderabad: order before 4 pm, delivery next day before 4 pm.
  • Sweets & snacks — Outside Hyderabad: order before 4 pm, dispatch next day before 4 pm.
  • Naturally grown essentials within Hyderabad & within India: same day (subject to availability).

Delivery

Delivery timelines may vary depending on your location and the availability of products. We work with reliable delivery partners to ensure timely delivery; however, delays due to weather, traffic, public holidays, or unforeseen circumstances may occur.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before you confirm your order.

Order Acceptance

Please inspect your order upon delivery. If you receive a damaged, incorrect, or incomplete order, kindly contact us within 24 hours with your order details and photographs, where applicable.

Delivery Availability

Delivery services are currently available only in selected locations. Availability may vary based on your delivery address.
